# T-160 · First real-application adoption

## Goal

Meet the use metric (§7): the router replaces hand-written routing in at least one real
application.

## Scope

- Identify the application and its current routing logic.
- Migrate it; compare cost, quality and routing behaviour before and after.
- Feed friction back into the requirements and docs.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The application's hand-written routing is removed and replaced by the router in production.
- [ ] Findings are filed as tasks or PRD changes.
